English Muffin Pizzas Recipe

⬅Home

Description

English muffin pizzas are a fun and easy way to use up leftover English muffins. They're perfect for a quick breakfast or brunch option.

Ingredients

  1. 4 English muffins, split in half
  2. 1 cup pizza sauce
  3. 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  4. 1 cup sliced pepperoni or other toppings of your choice
  5. 1 teaspoon dried oregano (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  2. Place the English muffin halves on a baking sheet, cut side up.
  3. Spread a spoonful of pizza sauce on each muffin half.
  4. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  5. Add your desired toppings, such as pepperoni, vegetables, or herbs.
  6. Sprinkle with dried oregano if desired.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  8. Remove from the oven and let cool for a few minutes before serving.
