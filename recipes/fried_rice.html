Fried Rice Recipe

⬅Home

Description

Fried rice is a versatile and flavorful dish that combines cooked rice with a variety of ingredients, such as vegetables, proteins, and seasonings. It is a popular choice for using up leftover rice and can be customized to suit different tastes and dietary preferences.

Ingredients

  1. ⅔ cup chopped baby carrots
  2. ½ cup frozen green peas
  3. 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  4. 1 clove garlic, minced, or to taste (Optional)
  5. 2 learge eggs
  6. 3 cups leftover cooked and chilled white rice
  7. 1 tablespoon soy sauce, or more to taste
  8. 2 teaspoons sesame oil, or to taste

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
  3. Push the rice to the side of the pan and scramble the eggs in the center.
  4. Add the baby carrots and green peas to the pan and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Add the cooked rice, soy sauce, and sesame oil to the pan.
  6. Stir everything together until well combined and heated through.
